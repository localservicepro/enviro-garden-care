02 · Project objective

What this engagement achieves

  • Own "Lawn Mowing Gold Coast" from the north. The head term is 320 searches a month at difficulty 10 — winnable — and the homepage already half-targets it. Re-cut the page so the keyword sits in the H1, title and first paragraph with the northern suburbs named alongside it.
  • Give Google 9+ pages instead of 3. Every competitor on page one has a page per service and a page per suburb. The client has one page trying to rank for ten services across nineteen suburbs.
  • Write the 18 missing suburbs into the site. Coomera, Ormeau, Helensvale, Hope Island, Upper Coomera — zero mentions each today. Nobody in those suburbs can find this business by searching.
  • Add the machine-readable layer. LocalBusiness, Service and FAQ schema, sitemap, and a mobile page that becomes usable in under 3 seconds instead of 6.

What they rank for that the client doesn't

  • Suburb pages. Fox alone ranks for Windaroo landscaping, Nerang, Gold Coast mowing services — each from a dedicated URL. The client has zero suburb URLs. This is the structural gap and it's the whole story.
  • Lawn-care questions. Pulse pulls traffic from "how to get rid of bindis" (70/mo), "best grass for Australian lawns" (90/mo) and clover removal (480/mo). The client has three plain-text FAQs.
  • Nobody owns the quiet/battery angle. "lawn mower noise gold coast" and "lawn mowing sunday gold coast" are live queries with no dedicated answer on page one.
  • Directories dominate mid-page. Airtasker, Localsearch, Hipages and Facebook groups hold positions 5–13. A proper service page with schema and reviews outranks a directory listing for a suburb term — that's the open door.
  • Authority gap is real but small in absolute terms. 63 referring domains gets a one-pager to position 7. The client needs local citations, a GBP link, and a handful of Gold Coast business links — not hundreds.

14 · Why this strategy works

The case in six lines

  • Fixes the five critical audit issues first — suburbs, service pages, schema, thin content, authority — the things actually blocking rankings
  • Targets a head term with real volume (320/mo) at a difficulty of 10, not a vanity term or a zero-volume one
  • Builds on a site Google already half-trusts: position 12 for Pimpama with nothing on the page targeting it
  • Matches the structure every page-one competitor already uses — page per service, page per suburb — and adds the schema most of them skip
  • Owns a differentiator nobody else on page one can claim: quiet, fume-free, battery-powered mowing at no extra cost
  • Scales across all 19 suburbs of the Northern Gold Coast without the pages competing with each other
