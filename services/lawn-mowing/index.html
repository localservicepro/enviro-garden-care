What every mow includes

  • Cut at the right height for your grass — we do not scalp buffalo or couch through a Brisbane summer
  • Edges trimmed along fences, paths, driveway, beds and around the letterbox
  • Paths, drive and patio blown down so the clippings leave with us
  • Clippings and green waste taken away — nothing left filling your wheelie bin
  • Gates shut, pets kept in mind, and a message when we are done if you are out

Domestic, acreage and commercial

  • Domestic blocks — standard suburban yards across the southside, on a schedule that changes with the season
  • Acreage mowing Brisbane — lifestyle lots and larger holdings cut with ride-on gear and slashed where it has got away
  • Commercial mowing Brisbane — body corporate grounds, strata, childcare, rentals and small industrial sites on a maintenance contract
  • One-off and end-of-lease cuts — inspections, sales, or a block nobody has touched since the last wet season

Schedules that match Brisbane grass

  • Weekly or fortnightly, October to March — couch, kikuyu and buffalo run hard through the storm season
  • Fortnightly to monthly, April to September — growth slows and so does the cost
  • Holiday cover so you are not coming home to a paddock

Lawn Mowing — your questions

Straight answers on price, coverage and what is included.

Do you mow lawns in Mount Gravatt and Mount Gravatt East?

Yes. A1 Lawn Care is based at 1593 Logan Rd, Mount Gravatt QLD 4122, and Mount Gravatt, Mount Gravatt East and Upper Mount Gravatt are on our weekly run, along with Wishart, Holland Park, Tarragindi, Coorparoo and Greenslopes.

How much does lawn mowing cost in Brisbane?

Lawn mowing in Brisbane is quoted per visit on the size of the lawn, the access, and how long it has been since the last cut. Regular fortnightly clients pay less per visit than one-off jobs, and an overgrown first cut costs more than the visits that follow. Call 0456 198 080 for a price on your block.

Do I need to be home when you mow?

No. As long as we can reach the lawn and any side gates are unlocked, we mow, tidy up and let you know it is finished. Most of our regular clients are at work when we visit.

Do you do commercial and acreage mowing?

Yes. We mow body corporate and strata grounds, rental portfolios and small industrial sites on a maintenance schedule, and we have the gear for acreage and lifestyle blocks across Brisbane's south side, Logan and the Redlands.
